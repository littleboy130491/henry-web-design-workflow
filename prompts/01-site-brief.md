# 01 — Refine the Site Brief

**Reads:** everything in `inputs/`
**Writes:** `outputs/01-site-brief.md`

## Task
Turn the raw client material in `inputs/` into one clear, structured site brief that every later step can rely on.

## Steps
1. Read every file in `inputs/` (documents, notes, images, links). If an existing website URL is given, fetch it and note what's there.
2. Extract and organize the facts. Separate what the client **stated** from what you **inferred** — label inferences.
3. Spot gaps, contradictions, and vague statements ("we want it modern", "for everyone"). Turn each into a specific question.

## Output structure
- **Company snapshot** — name, what they do (one sentence), location/market, years in business, size.
- **Offer** — products/services, pricing model (if given), what makes them different.
- **Business goal of the site** — the one primary conversion (call, booking, inquiry, purchase, sign-up) and secondary goals.
- **Audience (raw)** — who the client says they serve. (Refined in step 02.)
- **Brand** — existing logo, colors, fonts, tone of voice, brand assets available.
- **Competitors** — named or found, with URLs.
- **Content available** — copy, photos, testimonials, case studies, certifications, stats.
- **Technical** — platform/stack (static HTML, Framer, WordPress/Elementor, etc.), languages, integrations (forms, booking, WhatsApp, analytics), domain/hosting.
- **Constraints** — deadline, budget level, must-haves, must-avoids.
- **Success metrics** — how the client will judge the site.
- **Open questions for the client** — numbered, specific, ready to send.

Keep it tight: bullets, no filler. Someone who has never met the client should understand the project in 3 minutes.
