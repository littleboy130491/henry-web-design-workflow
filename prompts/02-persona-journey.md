# 02 — Ideal Customer Persona & Journey

**Reads:** `outputs/01-site-brief.md`, `inputs/`
**Writes:** `outputs/02-persona-journey.md`

## Task
Define who the site is really for, how they move from problem to purchase, and exactly where the website fits in that journey.

## Steps
1. From the brief, identify the **primary** persona (the buyer who brings the most value) and at most 1–2 secondary personas. Don't create personas just to fill slots.
2. If useful, research the market (reviews of competitors, forums, social comments, search queries) to ground the persona in real language. Cite sources.
3. Map the journey and mark the website's role at each stage.

## Output structure
For each persona:
- **Who** — role/life situation, context, decision power (do they buy, or recommend to someone who buys?).
- **Trigger** — the moment they start looking for a solution.
- **Problem in their own words** — 3–5 phrases they'd actually type or say.
- **Desired outcome** — what "solved" looks like to them.
- **Objections & fears** — why they hesitate (price, trust, effort, risk, time).
- **Decision criteria** — what they compare on.
- **Where they come from** — Google, Instagram, referral, ads, marketplace, WhatsApp, etc. and on which device.

**Journey map** (table): Stage → What they think/feel → What they do → Question they need answered → Website's job at this stage → Page/section that does it.
Stages: Unaware/Trigger → Searching → Comparing → Deciding → Converting → After purchase/Referral.

**Website's role summary** — 3–5 sentences: which stages the website must win, which happen elsewhere (e.g. WhatsApp chat, sales call), and the hand-off between them.
