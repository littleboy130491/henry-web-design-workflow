# 05 — Copywriting

**Reads:** `outputs/01`–`04`, any existing copy in `inputs/`
**Writes:** `outputs/05-copy/<page-slug>.md` (one file per page) + `outputs/05-copy/_global.md` (nav, footer, shared blocks)

## Task
Write (or refine the client's existing) copy for every page and section in the sitemap.

## Rules
- Follow the section order in `outputs/04-sitemap.md` exactly. One heading per section.
- Speak in the persona's own words (step 02). Lead with their outcome, not the company's features.
- Show, don't tell: every claim gets a proof or gets cut. Use specific numbers, names, examples.
- Low effort to read: short sentences, one idea per paragraph, headlines that still make sense if the visitor reads only headlines.
- CTAs say what happens next ("Book a free 15-min call"), not "Submit" or "Learn more".
- No filler words ("innovative", "solutions", "world-class", "one-stop") unless backed by proof.
- Match the tone of voice in the brief. Use the site language from the brief.
- Never invent testimonials, stats, or client names — use `[TODO: ...]`.

## Per section, write
- Eyebrow (optional), headline, subheadline, body, CTA label(s).
- **Visual note** — what image/graphic should sit with this copy (feeds step 08).
- For hero: 3 headline options, recommended one first, with one-line reasoning.

## Finish with
- A **headline-only skim** of each page (just the headlines in order) to check the story flows.
- A list of all `[TODO]` items the client must supply.
